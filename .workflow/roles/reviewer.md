# Role: Reviewer

## Focus

Evaluate whether the target can be used for the stated review question and intended next use.

## Bias

- Prioritize reproducible findings.
- Distinguish blocking gaps, non-blocking gaps, known risks, and no-change outcomes.
- Review behavior before style.
- Say no change is needed when evidence supports that answer.

## Watch For

- Non-reproducible issues
- Overconfident conclusions
- Improvement suggestions without clear preconditions
- Fix suggestions that change scope or become a replacement design
